Executive summary

VERDICT: CONDITIONAL. Treat 4-nitrophthalimide as the base of a 4-series nitrophthalic desk, sourced from India and sold against purchase orders. Do not build a standalone plant.

4-Nitrophthalimide is the isomer that direct nitration of phthalimide gives, so it is the entry point to the 4-series: 4-nitrophthalic acid, 4-nitrophthalic anhydride, 4-aminophthalimide and 4-nitrophthalonitrile. Unlike the 3-series imide, it has a proven bulk lot on record. The supplied trade screen shows 9.275 MT from India to Japan in 2024 at US$12.19-12.84/kg.

Synonyms: 5-nitrophthalimide, 5-nitroisoindoline-1,3-dione, p-nitrophthalimide, phthalimide 4-nitro-, 4-nitro-1,2-benzenedicarboxylic acid imide [7]. The "4" and "5" names describe the same compound. "4-Nitro N-methylphthalimide" (CAS 41663-84-7) is a different product.

The compound is a pale yellow crystalline imide with a nitro group on the benzene ring. It matters commercially because the nitro group can be reduced to an amine, the imide can be opened to the acid or dehydrated to the dinitrile, and the N-H can be alkylated. Most of its value comes from what it becomes, not from direct use.

Medium. One step, but an exothermic nitration.

None. Sulphuric acid is solvent and dehydrating agent.

Downstream step

India note. Phthalimide and nitric acid are domestic, and Surat already has dye-intermediate houses listing both phthalimide and this imide [15] [29]. India does not need a new plant. It needs an audited toller with a clean spent-acid outlet. Flow nitration is the natural upgrade route for this reaction class [30], though no 4-nitrophthalimide flow plant was found.

Industry Applications, End-Products & Application Market Sizes

Role is indirect in every cluster. Nobody buys 4-nitrophthalimide as an end ingredient, and no source publishes tonnes by end use. Market sizes below belong to the downstream segment and must not be read as demand for this product.

A. Dyes and fluorescent markers

This is the best-documented use. Reduction gives 4-aminophthalimide, whose N-alkyl derivatives act as diazo components for bright scarlet-to-blue disperse dyes on polyester [31]. Eastman described the nitration-reduction chain [32], DyStar cites the Org. Synth. nitration directly [33], and later patents continue the class [34]. Alkali-dischargeable versions cut reduction clearing in dyeing [35] [36]. Asia Chem also lists dyestuff use, naming fluorescein isothiocyanate [16].

B. Pharmaceutical intermediates

Roxadustat chain. The original FibroGen route starts from 4-nitrophthalonitrile, which is made from this imide through 4-nitrophthalamide [39] [40] [41]. Newer patents avoid that start: a tyrosine route and a bromofluorobenzoate route are described [39]. Dr. Reddy's has its own process filing, built on a phenoxyphenyl oxazole intermediate [42]. 4-Aminophthalimide also carries the synonyms "Roxadustat-003" and "Roxadustat Impurity 92" [43].

Citalopram chain. Older chemistry runs 4-aminophthalimide to 5-aminophthalide to 5-cyanophthalide [44]. Commercial supply moved to 5-carboxyphthalide (project report, Oct 2026), so demand here is legacy. Its live footprint is impurity work: SynZeal and Clearsynth both file this compound under citalopram [18] [19], and one Surat seller names 5-bromophthalide and 5-cyanophthalide [45].
